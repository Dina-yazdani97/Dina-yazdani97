## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I’ve always been curious about the world around me, especially the patterns hidden in nature, climate, and geographical data. My background in geography and climatology gradually led me to programming, data analysis, and creating maps that help tell the stories behind the data.

I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning new tools, and turning complex data into something clear and meaningful. I also love sharing what I learn with others through teaching R for geospatial analysis and being part of the R community.

Here on GitHub, I share my research, mapping projects, and experiments with R, Python, and data science. I'm always happy to learn from others, exchange ideas, and collaborate on projects that help us better understand our world. 🌍
